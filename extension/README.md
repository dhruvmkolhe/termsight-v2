# TermSight Chrome / Brave / Edge Extension

Instant 1-click Terms of Service and Privacy Policy auditor for your browser.

## How to Install (Takes 30 seconds):
1. Open Google Chrome, Brave, or Microsoft Edge.
2. Navigate to `chrome://extensions/` (or `edge://extensions/`).
3. Turn ON **"Developer mode"** in the top-right corner.
4. Click **"Load unpacked"**.
5. Select this `extension` folder (`c:\Users\dhruv\Downloads\agon-agent_1-51055aa1\extension`).
6. Done! The **TermSight** icon will appear in your browser extension toolbar.

## How to Use:
1. Whenever you are on any company's Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, or EULA page, click the **TermSight** extension icon.
2. Click **"Audit This Page"**.
3. It will extract and pipe the full legal text into TermSight for immediate plain-English analysis.
